## Design Context

### Users
Thai upper-secondary students (ม.4–6) learning environmental awareness and pollution; teachers upload and manage course resources. Students read on phones and classroom computers and ask questions grounded in teacher materials.

### Brand Personality
Clear · encouraging · trustworthy. The creator confirmed a light, readable, warm classroom experience, then refined it to a simple green-and-blue theme. Product name: Eco-Critical AI Chatbot.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light paper surfaces, forest green navigation, restrained blue AI accents. Quiet educational editorial layout, clear Thai text, generous breathing room. No gradients, glowing effects, decorative charts, or excessive cards.
Typography procedure: reflex choices Inter, DM Sans and Newsreader were rejected. Google Fonts Thai catalog reviewed; Bai Jamjuree for headings and Sarabun for extended reading evoke classroom materials while keeping Thai legible. Fonts are served locally.

### Design Principles
1. Teacher materials are the source of truth; distinguish missing evidence from an answer.
2. Encourage critical thinking about causes, affected groups, evidence and feasible action.
3. Keep course access, lesson uploads and source citations understandable.
4. Use real server state; never invent enrollment, progress, documents or AI responses.
5. Support narrow screens, keyboard focus and reduced motion.

## Product redesign — user correction, 5 November 2026
The initial hero/globe/slogan/template layout was explicitly rejected. Never restore it. The subsequent full brief requests a functional EdTech product: compact welcome, four illustrated topic modules, lesson reader with contextual tutor, clear teacher/student navigation, mobile bottom nav, three working learning activities, and an explicitly labeled demo where live AI cannot be verified. Use the exact user green/blue palette and Lucide icons. No brochure, giant hero, filler marketing sections, invented progress, or unmarked simulated AI. Preserve React/Vite and the imported AnythingLLM backend. Demo persistence is browser-local and retrieval is extractive, not Gemini.
